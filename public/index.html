🌐 About Us

MediLoc

“Your Health, Just Around the Corner.”

MediLoc is a digital platform designed to make healthcare access faster, easier, and more reliable by helping patients find medicines at nearby pharmacies instantly.

🎯

Our Mission

To make healthcare access faster, easier, and more reliable by connecting people with nearby pharmacies and medical stores.

👁️

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted digital health locator platform, ensuring no patient struggles to find medicines when they need them the most.

📖 Our Story

MediLoc started as a simple idea during an engineering project: “What if patients could instantly locate medicines at nearby pharmacies instead of wasting time searching?” With that thought, we built MediLoc – a smart platform bridging the gap between patients and pharmacies using technology.

💡 Core Values

  • Trust – Reliability in every service
  • Innovation – Leveraging technology for better healthcare
  • Customer-First – Prioritizing patient needs
  • Transparency – Honest & open information
  • Accessibility – Easy healthcare for all
